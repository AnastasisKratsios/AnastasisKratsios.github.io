Geometric Foundations of AI

Applications to: Finance, Control, and PDE

I investigate the analytic and statistical foundations of deep learning. By understanding the behaviour of these models, I can rigorously modify them to efficiently encode the structures arising in: finance, stochastic processes, games, and PDEs.
